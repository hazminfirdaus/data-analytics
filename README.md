# Data Analytics: OSMI Mental Health in Tech Survey

This repository contains the coursework for the CIS4010-N **Data Analytics** module:
- **ICA** – slide presentation (project plan)
- **ECA** – full analysis of the OSMI Mental Health in Tech Survey

## 1. Project Overview

**Main research question:**

> Can we predict whether a person will seek mental-health treatment (`treatment`) based on demographic, workplace, and perception-based attributes from the OSMI Mental Health in Tech dataset?

Supporting questions explore:
- Demographic and organisational factors related to treatment.
- The role of workplace support, benefits, and stigma.
- Natural clusters of employees based on mental-health–related characteristics.
